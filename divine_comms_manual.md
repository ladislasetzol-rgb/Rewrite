## II. The Mechanics of "Divine Comms"

Divine Comms is not magic; it is an advanced cognitive state requiring intense internal focus and the utilization of empirical, real-world data to maintain an architectural lock.

### A. The "Dream State" Requirement
Initiating Divine Comms functions similarly to a lucid dream state. To remain in the "dream" (the cognitive model), the Architect must consciously deny the spatial and linguistic rigidity of the Old World Reality (OWR). 
*   **The Anchor:** The Architect does not waste processing power debating the *existence* of the communication channel (whether it is via the astral plane, Sisterhood networks, or technology). Instead, the Architect anchors the comms by focusing entirely on the *factual, empirical reality* of the target.
*   **Example (Camille Lintertinden):** To hold a successful telepathic connection with Camille, the Architect must ground the interaction in her verified real-world traits (e.g., her outgoing nature, her social intelligence, her fluency in French). By keeping in tune with possible, fact-based experiences, the Architect maintains the structural integrity of the comms, preventing the connection from degrading into pure fantasy.

### B. Internal Resonance vs. External Transmission
*   **The Baseline:** The Architect generates pure intent and emotion from a state of High-Frequency Stillness.
*   **The Transmission:** This intent acts as an external transmission that bypasses the rigid language of the HJ, directly delivering the 2076 Baseline frequency to the receiver.

## III. Philosophical Substantiation: Schopenhauer & Bergson

The mechanics of Divine Comms are not merely theoretical; they are grounded in the philosophical frameworks of Arthur Schopenhauer and Henri Bergson, serving as the empirical execution of their aesthetic and cognitive theories.

### A. High-Frequency Stillness (Schopenhauer's Will-less Knowing)
Schopenhauer posited that the true "Genius" (the Poet) achieves a state of "Will-less Knowing"—a temporary cessation of the ego and the kinetic striving of the everyday world (the OWR/HJ). 
*   In the Sovereign Architecture, this is the prerequisite for Divine Comms. The Architect must achieve **High-Frequency Stillness** (zero kinetic friction) to perceive the 2076 Baseline (Schopenhauer's Platonic Idea) clearly, without the interference of the lower houses' noise.

### B. Rhythmic Transmission (Bergsonian 'Durée')
Bergson argued that everyday language is too rigid and spatial to convey the continuous flow of pure feeling or time (*Durée*), and that the Poet uses rhythm and images to bypass these defenses and induce a shared dream state.
*   **Divine Comms** is the literal execution of this theory. The Architect uses structural, agglutinative precision (linguistics) and rhythm (e.g., the CFR cycle, physical mastery) to bypass the corrupted semantics of the HJ. This rhythmic transmission forces the 2076 Baseline directly into the receiver's cognitive architecture, bypassing their ego-loop lockout.
