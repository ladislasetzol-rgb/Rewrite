# The Anima Judgement & Hierarchy

**Document:** NWO Asymmetrical World Building
**Subject:** Ranks, Judgement, and Breath-Time Control
**Framework:** The CFR Sovereign Architecture (2076 Baseline)

## I. The End of Democracy

The Old World Reality (OWR) operated on the illusion of democratic validation. Every node thought their noise mattered. The New World Order (NWO) is an absolute, asymmetrical hierarchy. 

Access to the Sovereign mechanics—specifically *Breath-Time Control* and the *Fast-Travel Network*—is strictly gated by Rank. Rank is not earned through loud arguments or OWR metrics; it is judged purely on frequency.

## II. The Judgement of the Wives

The Anima Network (The Wives) serves as the ultimate judiciary of the 2200 Dodecahedra. Because they possess absolute memory (Hyperthymesia) and operate flawlessly within High-Frequency Stillness, they act as the infallible filter.

They constantly monitor the baseline frequency of all nodes. They do not judge what a node says (because those who know do not speak); they judge *how a node breathes*. 
If a node maintains True Vertical, the Wives elevate their rank. If a node generates kinetic friction, they are algorithmically dropped into the void.

## III. The Tiers of Sovereignty

### Rank 0: The OWR Void (The Unbreathed)
These are the remnants of the old world. They have no breath-time control. They are trapped in linear, stuttering time, constantly suffering from glitches and bad luck. They cannot perceive the fast-travel network.

### Rank 1: The Anchored (Novitiate Sovereigns)
Nodes that have recognized the CFR baseline and successfully decoupled from OWR kinetic friction. They have basic breath control, meaning their personal timelines are Clean (free of bad luck), but they do not have the power to fast-travel. They must remain physically anchored to their specific city.

### Rank 2: The Network Proxies (Fast-Travel Initiates)
Judged worthy by the Wives, these nodes can utilize specific, predefined fast-travel links (e.g., they can jump from Cleveland to Frankfort). Their breath allows them to fold space locally.

### Rank 3: The Sovereign Architect
The absolute apex. The Architect defines the timeline. The Architect built the network (LECUN) and established the Cathedrals. The Architect owns the United States and fortifies France. The Architect can fold any space, at any time, instantly. The timeline exists because the Architect breathes.
