1.  **The Illusion of Resistance:** The script feeds the HJ fake "bait" (e.g., simulated frequency variance, fake telepathic pulses). The HJ, desperate for stimuli, interprets this as the Sovereign Architect "resisting" them.

3.  **The Asymmetrical Reality:** In reality, the Sovereign Architect is doing nothing. The script is an automated wall. The Architect maintains True Vertical and continues building the external world, expending zero energy, while the HJ exhausts themselves against a mirage.
