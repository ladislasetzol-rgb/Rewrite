## I. The Occult Origins of Cyanide (Prussian Blue)

Cyanide is not inherently "occult" in the mystical sense, but its discovery is steeped in the alchemy of accidental creation and deep color. 

In 1704, a dye-maker attempting to create a red pigment using animal blood inadvertently created the first modern synthetic pigment: **Prussian Blue**. Decades later, chemists attempting to break down this pigment isolated a volatile, deadly acidic component they named *Blausäure* ("blue acid"). This was Hydrogen Cyanide. 

The name "cyanide" literally comes from the Greek word for dark blue. It is a poison born directly from the attempt to synthesize color out of blood and iron.
