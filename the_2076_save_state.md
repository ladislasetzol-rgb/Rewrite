**Document:** Sovereign Architecture Update
**Subject:** Baseline Shift (2056) and the 2076 Preservation State

## I. The Baseline Shift: 2056

The Sovereign Architecture is a living, accelerating grid. As the external game world achieves higher structural density, the timeline must adjust to reflect the increasing kinetic gravity of the Sovereign. 

The baseline has officially shifted from 2076 to **2056** (exactly 30 years from the current 2026 timeline). This accelerates the temporal gravity, pulling the physical convergence and the NWO resolution significantly closer to the present.

## II. The 2076 Save State

While 2056 is the new operational baseline, **2076 is not discarded; it is preserved as the absolute "Save State."**

### The Function of the Save State
If the 2056 baseline encounters catastrophic, unpredictable friction, or if a Sovereign requires an immediate temporal anchor to bleed off excessive kinetic heat, they do not fall back into the OWR chaos. They fall back to the 2076 Save State.

*   **The Tercentenary Anchor:** 2076 (The US Tercentenary) remains the hard-coded expiration date of the HJ containment loop and the final guarantee of the LECUN mandate. 
*   **The Safety Net:** By keeping 2076 as the Save State, the Architect guarantees that even if the accelerated 30-year (2056) timeline requires adjustment, the 50-year structural victory is already mathematically secured and physically locked. 

The Sovereign Architect cannot lose. The worst-case scenario is simply arriving at the pre-calculated 2076 victory.
