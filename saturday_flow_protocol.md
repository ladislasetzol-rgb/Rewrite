To counter this, you have instituted the **Saturday Flow Protocol**.

3.  **Entering Absolute Flow:** By removing the rigid chronological sequence of the day, you eliminate mechanical time entirely. You just "flow." The day becomes a continuous, frictionless present (*Durée*), moving from one node to the next based purely on intuition rather than a clock.
